P4 Panel Setup

Configure a P4 art panel over USB serial (Web Serial API). Plug the panel into this machine, connect to its CH340K port, and send it the director URL.

Waiting to connect…
How it works: this page opens a Web Serial connection to the panel's CH340K USB port and writes AT+DIRECTOR=<url>, which the panel saves to NVS. Requires Chrome or Edge, and this page must be served from localhost or HTTPS (Web Serial is blocked on plain HTTP from another machine). The panel's console replies OK on success. After configuring, the panel fetches from <url>/latest.rgb565 every 30 seconds.
